Financial Support

We Back Our Teams

FWNS-MD actively supports affiliated clubs and teams participating in sports tournaments and community activities across Maryland and beyond.

As per our Bylaws and Sports Participation & Support Policy, FWNS-MD provides up to $1,000 in financial support per activity, subject to Executive Committee approval.

Support may cover tournament entry fees, travel costs, and other approved sports activity expenses. All requests must be submitted in advance and meet the membership requirements below.

What Support Can Cover

  • ✓ Tournament entry & registration fees
  • ✓ Travel and transportation costs
  • ✓ Equipment and uniform costs (if approved)
  • ✓ Other approved activity expenses
Up to $1,000
per activity · subject to EC approval
Eligibility Requirement

Membership Requirement for Support

To qualify for FWNS-MD financial support, teams must meet the following membership requirements as per our Bylaws and Sports Policy.

👥

Adult Teams

  • ✓ At least 2/3 of all team members must be current FWNS-MD members in good standing
  • ✓ A full player list with membership status must be submitted with the request
🧒

Youth Teams (Under 18)

  • ✓ At least 2/3 of youth players must have a parent or guardian who is a current FWNS-MD member
  • ✓ A full player list with parent/guardian membership status must be submitted
📋

Required when submitting: A complete player roster with each member's FWNS-MD membership status must be included in the support request form.
